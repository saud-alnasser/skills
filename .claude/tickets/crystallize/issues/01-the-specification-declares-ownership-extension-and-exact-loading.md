## Problem

The specification's systems parts treat every installed instruction file as
repository-owned and healable, describe the norm-plus-essay prose form as the
house style, and let each stage judge which of its guides to open. All four
decisions of this effort contradict that text, and every downstream ticket
derives from the specification, so building any of them first would ship
surfaces the normative document disowns.

## Outcome

The specification states the ownership model (framework-owned law, verbatim
install, hash audit, extension points, loud deviations), the norm form (each
norm a checkable imperative with a one-line why; essays stay in the framework
repository and are not installed), and exact loading (mandatory whole-list stage
loads; the always-on core selected by the drift test, with its four members
named). The sections the new text falsifies are amended in the same change, per
the precedence rule — which is also why ADRs 0073–0075 sit at `proposed` until
this ticket lands: they, and 0078, move to `accepted` here, where the
specification stops disagreeing with them.

## Acceptance

- A reader of the specification alone can answer: which files declare an owner,
  what `framework` ownership binds a session to, how a repository varies a fixed
  policy, and what a deviation is.
- The specification names the always-on core's members and the test that
  selects them.
- No surviving sentence in the specification says or implies that a stage
  chooses which of its guides to read, or that an installed policy is the
  repository's to heal.
- The quality-gates part carries the determinism conformance: a fixed-core
  procedure is computed or names its judgement; conversion is manifest-driven
  with fire-checked guards; extension points are census-derived, never
  invented; a deviation's age is computed and one release without a
  disposition fails the audit.
