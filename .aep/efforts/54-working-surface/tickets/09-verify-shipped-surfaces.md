when the claim is removed. The suite judges what ships, never an installed tree.

## Constraints

**Verify checks shipped surfaces only** — `src/`, and what a release installs. It
never asserts over `.aep/`, which is this repository's installed output and is
regenerated by ticket 10.

Every assertion is seen to fail against a tree with its subject removed, and the
perturbation is confirmed to have removed the subject before the red is believed
(`[[rules/authoring]]`).

Assert on the claim, not on a sentence. A guard matching one phrasing passes the
moment somebody rewords the line it was watching.

## Notes

Ticket 08 covers git behaviour and the close-out lifecycle against real
worktrees. This ticket covers text and shape. The split is so that neither is a
context's worth of two unrelated kinds of assertion.

The effort 51 and version-control assertions are over repository-owned files
rather than payload. They are asserted because this effort changed them, not
because verify audits repository-owned artifacts in general.
