## Constraints

- **`.aep/` is output** ([[contexts/repository]]). Reinstall; do not edit it to
  match.
- The adapter is generated from each artifact's own heading and `use-when`. This
  effort changes neither for any skill, so an adapter diff is a signal that
  something else moved and is worth reading before committing.
- **Run `node src/scripts/release.mjs 3.3.0`.** This replaces a constraint that
  said the opposite, and the correction is ticket 04's finding: as written, "no
  release is cut here" made criterion 10 unreachable for every ticket in this
  effort that edits a stamped payload artifact, because `verify.mjs` fails any
  such edit until the baseline moves. Three things settle it together. `specs.md`
  rule 3 says an amendment bumps the version and the release stamps every
  protocol-owned artifact it changes, and this effort amends `specs.md`.
  [[rules/authoring]] calls a re-cut baseline a deliberate act rather than
  housekeeping, and at the close the tree genuinely is the new reference point.
  And criterion 10 requires a green suite.

  **3.3.0, not 4.0.0.** Effort 54 was also a breaking protocol change and landed
  as `3.2.0`. Major is reserved for an architecture generation, which 1.x, 2.0
  and 3.0 each were and this is not.

  Two tickets restamped mid-effort to get their own trees green, at `3.2.0` and
  by hand. The single re-cut here supersedes both, which is why `stamps.json`
  needs no reconciliation beyond running the command.

- **Cutting the version of record is not publishing.** Pushing a tag or
  publishing a release is a separate act and stays the human's
  ([[rules/version-control]]). Never push beyond the effort branch.
