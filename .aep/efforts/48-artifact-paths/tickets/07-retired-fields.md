## Relevant areas

`src/scripts/verify.mjs`, reading `RETIRED_FIELDS` from `src/scripts/contract.mjs` and `outsideFences` from the same module.

## Constraints

The allowlist stays at two files and each addition to it is argued. An allowlisted file can still make a false live claim and the guard will not see it, which is the known hole and is bounded only by the list staying small.

**No allowlisting of `AGENTS.md`.** It is the file that failed, and the whole point of the sentence-level scope is that the entrypoint is checked rather than excused.

`src/scripts/` is outside the scan. A script's object keys collide with field names — `scope.mjs` alone holds five `kind:` — and `RETIRED_FIELDS` itself lives there, so scanning scripts would check the list against its own definition.

`specs.md` is outside it too. It is the specification rather than shipped text, and requirement 8 governs what ships.

## Notes

The check cannot be "the string `aep` appears": `migration.md` exists to discuss exactly these fields. The colon is what distinguishes the frontmatter field from the word, what follows the colon is what distinguishes it from a label prefix, and the retirement clause is what distinguishes a description of the field from an instruction to write it.

**The plan was corrected on 2026-08-26 and this ticket was re-cut with it.** The rule it originally carried allowed a hit only inside a file whose subject is retirement, over an allowlist of five. Run against the corpus, it reported the corrected `AGENTS.md`, because the sentence requirement 9 exists to protect says `aep:` in order to say the field is gone. Evidence: `[[efforts/48-artifact-paths/evidence/research/retired-field-scan-corpus]]`. Two alternatives were put to the human and declined — scoping to the `##` section, which needs no allowlist and lets an unrelated mention of retirement excuse every live claim in the section, and pinning `AGENTS.md`'s sentence by a hand-written assertion, which is the pattern this effort exists to end.

The fixture in criterion 3 matters more than the `aep:` case. Passing on `aep:` proves the assertion runs; passing on a field nobody anticipated proves it generalises, and only the second is what requirement 8 asks for.
