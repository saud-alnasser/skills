# Template — research

Copy to `.aep/efforts/<effort>/evidence/research/<question-slug>.md`.

```markdown
---

---

# Question
One sentence, specific enough that an answer could be wrong about it.

# Sources
Each one named, with what it is and when it was read. Mark any that is a
secondary write-up rather than the thing itself.

# Findings
One per claim. Each carries its citation, and each says what it is true *of* —
the version, the date, the platform. A fact with no subject silently becomes a
claim about the present.

Distinguish, visibly:
  source        what the source says
  observation   what you saw
  interpretation what you take it to mean
  conclusion    what follows

# Conclusion
The answer. Findings only — never a decision, never a rule. If this changes the
design, that change is made deliberately in the spec.

# Not checked
What you did not look at, and what stayed open. A gap you name costs a line; a
gap you leave implicit reads as coverage.
```

**What you looked for and did not find is a finding.** Record it, or the next
investigation spends its budget rediscovering the same absence.
