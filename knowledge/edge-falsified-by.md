## falsified-by closes at one hop

The finding that falsified this record is wanted, so whoever opens it learns that
something contradicts it without having to open an index first. What *that*
finding cites is the finding's own business, on the same terms the forward edge
takes — this is one pair, read from either end, and a pair that closed further in
one direction than the other would return a different graph depending on which end
