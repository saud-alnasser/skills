## supersedes closes fully

A superseded decision without its successor is the wrong answer at any distance,
so the walk does not stop while the chain continues. A chain three replacements
long is the case the number is for: stopping at two returns a decision that was
already replaced, and returns it as though it were current.

Its opposite closes the same way, for the same reason read from the other end.
